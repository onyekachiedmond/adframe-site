Quick answers

FAQ

Do you do media buying?

No. We focus 100% on creative: strategy, scripts, and production. You or your media buyer keep control of the ad accounts, and we feed them a steady pipeline of tests.

Do AI creatives actually perform?

Yes. When they're built on real research and strong angles, AI creatives perform as well as traditional UGC and often better, because you can test far more variations in the same time.

Will the ads look fake or off-brand?

No. Most videos are built around your real footage, and every asset follows your brand guidelines. AI characters and voiceovers are chosen and directed to fit your audience.

Does this work for apps/software?

It's built for it. Screen recordings, AI character explainers, and animated formats work where traditional UGC struggles.

What if I have no footage / it's a new product?

No problem. We create net-new b-roll and full AI-produced creatives from scratch.

How much / turnaround?

Pricing depends on volume and scope, so book a call and we'll build a plan around your goals. Batches typically ship in 2–4 days.

Guarantee?

We don't lock you in: month-to-month, no long-term contracts, cancel anytime. We earn your business every batch.

Who's it for?

Ecom brands, apps, and software products that want to test more creative, find winners faster, and stop depending on creators.
