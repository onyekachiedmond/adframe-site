Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information AdFrame Media ("AdFrame", "we", "us") collects when you visit adframe.io or book a call with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Booking information. When you book a discovery call, you give us your name, email address, website or store URL, what you sell, your monthly ad spend range, and any notes you add. Bookings are handled by Cal.com and added to our Google Calendar, with a Google Meet link.
  • Messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Website usage. We may use analytics and advertising tools, such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, which use cookies or similar technologies to record pages visited, the device and browser used, approximate location, and whether a booking was made.

How we use it

  • To schedule and hold your discovery call, and to prepare for it.
  • To reply to your enquiries and, if we work together, to deliver our services.
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve it.
  • To measure and improve our own advertising, for example by showing ads to people who have visited the site.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the business, such as Cal.com (scheduling), Google Workspace (email, calendar and video calls), our website host, and, where enabled, Google Analytics and Meta. These providers process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies

Analytics and advertising tools may set cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, and you can control ad personalisation through your Google and Meta account settings. Blocking cookies does not stop you from using the site or booking a call.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, for the length of that relationship and any period required for legal or accounting purposes. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To make a request, email us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

International transfers

We work with clients around the world, and the services we use may store data in countries other than your own. Where this happens, we rely on those providers' safeguards for international data transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
